# Crisis Support Resources and Emergency Help FAQ

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, experiencing thoughts of self-harm, suicide, or severe psychiatric distress, please know that you do not have to carry this alone. Free, confidential support is available 24/7.

## Immediate Crisis Helplines (Global and Regional)
- United States & Canada:
  - Call or text **988** to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (English & Spanish).
  - Text **HOME** to **741741** to connect with the Crisis Text Line.
  - The Trevor Project (LGBTQ youth): Call 1-866-488-7386 or text START to 678-678.
- United Kingdom:
  - Call **111** for NHS mental health services.
  - Call **116 123** to reach the Samaritans (free 24/7).
  - Text **SHOUT** to **85258**.
- International & Worldwide:
  - Visit **https://findahelpline.com** for confidential, free mental health crisis helplines in over 130 countries.
  - Visit **https://www.befrienders.org** to find listening centers and emotional support worldwide.
  - In an emergency or severe medical situation, contact your local emergency emergency telephone services immediately (such as 911 in North America, 999 in the UK, 112 in the EU and India).

## What to Expect When Contacting a Helpline
Crisis hotlines are staffed by trained, compassionate listeners. You do not need to be in an extreme emergency to call or text—if you need someone supportive to listen without judgment, they are there to help you feel safe and heard.
